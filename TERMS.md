# Terms of Service (draft)

This is a draft for the operator to review before a public launch. Replace the contact placeholder.

## The service

Chapter Tracker Web is a free, best-effort notification service. It watches public weebcentral RSS feeds you ask it to watch and posts a Discord embed to webhooks you control when it detects a new chapter.

## No SLA

The service runs on free infrastructure with finite capacity. Checks may be delayed, and delivery can fail. There is no uptime or delivery guarantee. Capacity limits are shown in the app and may change.

## Free, with finite capacity

There is no paid plan, no card, and no advertising. Sign-ups are capped. When the cap is reached, new users join a waitlist. Existing users are not evicted to make room.

## Acceptable use

Do not use the service to spam a Discord server, to attack or overload third-party services, to send unlawful content, or to evade the capacity caps with multiple accounts. Webhook URLs must be ones you are authorized to post to.

## Your data

See `PRIVACY.md`. You can delete your account at any time.

## Content and affiliation

No chapter content or images are hosted. Alerts contain series and chapter metadata plus a link to weebcentral. This project is not affiliated with Discord or weebcentral, and it does not bypass paywalls, authentication, or anti-bot protections.

## Changes and termination

The operator may change limits, features, or these terms, and may suspend accounts that abuse the service. Continued use after a change means you accept it.
